Financial Ratio: Return on Equity (ROE)

Formula used for profitability analysis:

\[ ROE = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Shareholders' Equity}} \]

Example: \[ ROE = \frac{250}{1000} = 0.25 = 25\% \]
