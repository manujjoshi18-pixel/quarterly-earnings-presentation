Key Financial Formula

Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) valuation:

\[ V_0 = \sum_{t=1}^{N} \frac{CF_t}{(1 + r)^t} \]

Where:

  • \(V_0\): Present value of the asset
  • \(CF_t\): Cash flow at time \(t\)
  • \(r\): Discount rate
  • \(N\): Number of periods
Briefly explain how DCF is used internally for project and portfolio valuation. Connect this to the firm’s cost of capital.
